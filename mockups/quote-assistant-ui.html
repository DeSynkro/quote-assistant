Apply swell (material expansion)
Digging loosens material, so it takes more truck space. 30% means 8 m³ in the ground becomes 10.4 m³ loose. Off by default - leave it off if the volume is already loose.
Round up trucks
Shows the exact decimal and the rounded figure side by side.
GST
Internal estimate - GST off by default.
Plain-English guide
Swell - ground material expands when dug. Use roughly 20-30% for soil; rock and concrete can be more.
Settling - delivered material compacts after placement. Use 5-15% so you order enough loose material.
Volume only - ignores weight and density; just checks how much space the material takes.
Rounded trucks - you cannot hire part of a truck. The exact decimal stays visible so you can see the slack.
