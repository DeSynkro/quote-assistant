# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-25

Initial release.

### Added

- Offline browser estimator for earthmoving quotes.
- Removal sections with rectangle, uneven ends, manual area and manual volume shapes.
- Per-section swell, truck capacity, truck presets, pricing and rounding.
- Material deliveries by volume, weight or area × depth, with delivery pricing.
- Travel and fuel calculations.
- Combined spoil, material and travel totals with optional GST.
- Save, load, search, duplicate and delete jobs.
- Autosave drafts and a local restore path.
- Undo for saved job deletion.
- Export and import JSON backups for jobs and truck presets.
- Docker Compose LAN sharing for jobs and truck presets.
- Preferences for theme, measurement decimals, reduced motion and autosave.
- Dark, light and system themes.
- Responsive layout with a mobile estimate bar.
- Warning highlights and plain-language estimate summaries.
- Native packaging targets for Linux, Windows and Android.
